An illustrated one-page broadsheet summing up the world's news of Thursday 26 November 2026. Sixteen stories, every one verified as having happened or first been reported on 26 November 2026 in at least one time zone: three drawn lead panels (the Thanksgiving nor'easter on the US East Coast, the proposed US 15% tariff on Taiwan, and California's king tides), a big-number stat strip tied to individual stories, a region strip, a numbered eight-story grid with a drawn icon per story, a dotted world map with numbered pins matching the story numbers, an ALSO ON list for the five remaining stories, and a DATE CHECK box stating when the wires were last read and naming the 25 November stories that were deliberately left out. Every story names its source outlet.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) reads `entry.png` from this directory, checks it is a whole PNG or JPEG of at most 1 MiB and at most 1024x1024, then copies it to `/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` when that variable is set) and prints the result.

## Rebuild the image

`entry.png` is generated by `compose.py` (Python 3 + Pillow, system DejaVu fonts, no network):

```sh
python3 compose.py
```
